## The five posts

**1. The problem: what you found**
- **First line:** "Brown stain on the bedroom ceiling after the big rain. Here's why."
- **Shots:**
  - The ceiling stain, wide enough to show it's a ceiling. Nothing else from the bedroom in frame (no photos, beds or personal things). 5 seconds.
  - From the ground, the chimney and roof. Frame it so the house number, cars and neighbours' houses are out of shot. This is your before angle, so remember the spot. 5 seconds.
  - On the roof (Ray filming), a close-up of where the flashing has pulled away from the chimney, before anyone touches it. 5 seconds.
  - A close-up of the cracked sealant. 5 seconds.
- **What it shows:** the stain inside and its cause outside. That's the hook.

**2. The work: the main step**
- **First line:** "Old flashing out, new step flashing and counter-flashing in."
- **Shots:**
  - The old flashing coming off. Ray films and you work with both hands. Short clips, sped up later.
  - The new step flashing going in, course by course. Short clips.
  - The counter-flashing going on over it.
  - The reseal, a close-up of the sealant going on.
- **What it shows:** how the leak actually gets fixed, which most people never see.

**3. A tip: what the viewer can check safely**
- **First line:** "Got a stain near your chimney? Check this from the ground."
- **Shots:**
  - You on the ground pointing up at the chimney: "look here, where the roof meets the chimney."
  - Ray's close-up of the old flashing next to the pulled-away gap. Reuse it from Post 1, or grab a second take.
  - A shot of the ceiling stain as the "sign to look for".
- **What it shows:** signs it's time to call a pro, like a ceiling stain after rain, flashing you can see has lifted, or cracked sealant (with binoculars). Say it plainly: **don't go up on the roof yourself.**

**4. The result: before next to after**
- **First line:** "Same chimney, new flashing, sealed up."
- **Shots:**
  - On the roof, a close-up of the finished flashing from the same angle as the Post 1 close-up.
  - From the ground, the chimney from the same spot as the Post 1 ground shot.
  - A slow pan along the finished flashing and sealant.
- **What it shows:** the before and after side by side, so you can see the fix.
- Don't show or claim the ceiling stain is gone. That wasn't part of this job.

**5. A question people ask**
- **Question:** "Can't you just put more sealant on it?" *(check: do you hear this?)*
- **First line:** "People ask if more sealant would fix it. Here's my answer."
- **Answer:** [your answer in your own words]
- **Shots:**
  - You on the ground, talking to camera with the chimney behind you.
  - Cut to the cracked-sealant close-up from Post 1.
  - Cut to the new flashing from Post 4.
- **What it shows:** a straight answer to something customers wonder about, which builds trust.
